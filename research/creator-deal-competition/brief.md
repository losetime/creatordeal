# Research Brief: CreatorDeal Competitive Landscape Verification

## Research Question
Is CreatorDeal's product positioning accurate? Specifically:
1. Are there真正的竞争对手 in the "creator deal management" space?
2. Is the claim "no direct competitors" valid?
3. Are the differentiators (AI contract analysis, creator-specific) real?

## Scope
- **In**: Competitor analysis for creator/influencer deal management tools
- **In**: Verification of product positioning claims
- **Out**: General freelancing tools (Bonsai, HoneyBook) - only as comparison
- **Time frame**: Current market (2025-2026)

## Assumptions
- Target market: Content creators with 10K-1M followers
- Geography: Global, primarily English-speaking
- Price point: $9.90/month

## Depth Mode
Standard (3-5 sub-agents, 15+ sources)

## Date
2026-07-13

## Angles
1. Direct competitors - creator-specific deal/contract management tools
2. Indirect competitors - general freelancing tools with creator features
3. Market validation - do creators actually need this?
4. Differentiator verification - is AI contract analysis unique?
5. Pricing comparison - is $9.90/month competitive?
